Welcome to the Hilltop.

The Georgetown University Choir — comprising our Concert Choir and Chamber Singers — represents the choral tradition at one of the nation's oldest Catholic and Jesuit universities. We are proud of the vibrant musical community we have built and invite you to explore the talented singers, ambitious repertoire, and collaborative spirit that define our program.

Whether performing large-scale orchestral works, intimate a cappella masterpieces, or commissioning new compositions, our ensembles embody Georgetown's enduring commitment to the arts and to the transformative power of singing together.

Our Ensembles

Two Voices, One Program

SATB Choral Ensemble

Concert Choir

The Concert Choir is a large SATB ensemble performing sacred and secular works from all periods of music history. The Choir presents one major concert each semester, frequently collaborating with the Georgetown University Orchestra and guest artists. Open by audition to students, faculty, staff, and members of the University community. Host of the annual Messiah Sing-Along.

Chamber Singers

The Chamber Singers is a select group of mixed voices performing small choral masterworks from the Renaissance through the twenty-first century. Emphasis is placed on the art of a cappella singing, with repertoire spanning Josquin, Palestrina, Byrd, Poulenc, and living composers. The ensemble performs on campus, at venues across Washington, and on tour.
